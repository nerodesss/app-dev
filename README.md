# **MY FAVORITE MOVIE IS Gantz: O**
**"Your live have ended. How you use your new lives is entirely up to me"** 

## OVERVIEW
*Gantz: O* is a 3D CGI anime action horror film based on the Osaka Arc of the manga series GANTZ, written and illustrated by *Hiroyo Oku*

### Quick Facts
**Release Year:* 2016
**Genre:*Sci-Fi/ Action / Horror
**Animation Studio:* Digital Frontier
**Director:* Yasushi Kawamura & Kiiechi Sato

## Plot & Content 
The film follows *Masaru Kato*, a high school student who is fatally stabbed while trying to save a man at a subway station. Instead of dying, he wakes up in a room of with a mysterious black sphere known as *Gantz*.

### 1. The rules of Gantz  
* Deceased individuals are resurrected and given high - tech suits and futuristic waepons.
* Players are teleported to a battlefield to fight deadly yokai(monster/aliens).
* Each kill earns a point upon reaching *100 points*, a player can choose.
1. Erase their memory and go back to normal life.
2. Receive a powerful weapon upgrade.
3. Revive a fallen player from Gantz memory.

### 2. The Osaka Mission
Kato is sent to Osaka, where the local team has been fighting monsters for sport. They must face powerful mythological monsters, culminating in a battle against the form-shifting boss *Nurarihyon*.

## Key Characters
1. *Masaru Kato* — The compassionate protagonist fighting to return home to his younger brother.
2. *Reika Shimohira* — A veteran Tokyo team member who guides Kato.
3. *Anzu Yamasaki* — An Osaka team member who forms a close bond with Kato.
4. *Yoshikazu Suzuki* — An elderly Tokyo player who supports the team.

## Key Features & Themes

- [x] *Full 3D CGI Animation* — Photorealistic visuals and detailed action sequences.
- [x] *Yokai Mythology* — Reimagines classic Japanese folklore creatures (Nurarihyon, Tengu, Kraut) into alien threats.
- [x] *High-Stakes Combat* — Fast-paced battles using high-tech suits, Z-Guns, and Gantz bikes.
- [ ] PG-13 Friendly Content — Contains heavy violence and mature themes.
